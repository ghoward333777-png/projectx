Backups

A checked backup is taken when the application starts (if anything changed) and before and after every import. The newest 10 are kept.

How this application treats facts

  • Sources written by people only. Books, encyclopedias, newspapers, news agencies, government records, archives, museums. Web addresses of AI chat and AI-writing services are refused.
  • Status. sourced has at least one proper source; needs checking came from the old QueryBook without a citation; needs source has none yet.
  • Disagreements are shown, not settled. If two facts give different values for something that has one answer (a birth date, a capital), both are kept and marked disputed, each with its own sources.
  • The same fact twice is stored once, with all of its sources — more independent sources means better support.
  • Dates can be written as 1215, 15 June 1215, June 1215, 44 BC, c. 1200, 1990s or 12th century.
  • News headlines stay separate until you add them, and are then recorded as reported by their outlet.
  • No AI model is used, and nothing is sent anywhere except when you fetch a news feed you added.
