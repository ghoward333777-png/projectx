How learning works

  • New words arrive a few each day (you choose how many), most common words first, your own words before those.
  • Reviews follow a fixed schedule. Each correct answer moves a word up a box: you see it again after 1, 2, 4, 8, 16, 32, then 64 days. A miss brings it back today. A word is mastered once you answer it correctly at the 32-day step.
  • Practice modes: flashcards (you judge yourself), multiple choice, typing, listening (write what you hear) and speaking.
  • Checking is by rule. Case and spaces don't matter. A missing accent counts as right with a reminder; one wrong letter is shown as nearly right but counts as a miss.

Speech

  • Listening uses your computer's voices through the browser. If there is no voice for a language, the app uses espeak-ng when it is installed; otherwise it tells you how to add one (Windows: Settings → Time & language → Speech; macOS: System Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content).
  • Speaking uses your browser's speech recognition (Chrome, Edge, Safari). In Chrome and Edge the recording is processed online by the browser maker; nothing is sent by this application itself. Your answer counts when what was heard is at least 80% the same as the word.

Word lists

Known limits of the bundled lists: translations are in lower case (German nouns are normally capitalized), some are inflected forms, and the 1913 definitions use old wording. Add corrections as your own words.
