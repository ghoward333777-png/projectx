How answers are made

  • Whole numbers and fractions are exact: 0.1 + 0.2 is exactly 3/10.
  • Answers that cannot be exact (most square roots, sines, logarithms) are marked with ≈ and shown to 12 significant figures.
  • Unit conversions use the legal definitions (an inch is exactly 2.54 cm). Units of different kinds are refused, never forced.
  • Constants come from CODATA 2018; atomic weights from IUPAC. Each table names its source.
  • Nothing is sent to the internet and no AI model is used.
