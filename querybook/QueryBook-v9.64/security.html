Agent protection & deception

Every agent call, including OpenClaw's, passes through six layers. Traps catch attackers; the log records every decision in a form that can't be quietly altered.

The six layers

1 · Agent gatewayEach agent may only run its own allow-listed commands. Callers prove who they are with a secret token. Old or replayed requests are refused.
2 · DeceptionFake admin commands and fake keys, passwords and tokens that no real user ever touches. Any use is treated as an attack.
3 · Tripwires & honeypotsBait such as /.env, /admin, /debug/root_dump and fake "vault" agents, all serving synthetic data.
4 · AI-aware firewallBlocks prompt injection, jailbreaks, hidden-text tricks and self-modification requests. Rate-limits callers and spots automated scanning.
5 · ContainmentA tripped session is isolated and sees only a decoy world from then on, while QueryBook records its tools and moves. QueryBook never attacks back.
6 · Tamper-evident logEntries are hash-chained, and Merkle checkpoints are written once and can't be overwritten. They can be mirrored to another drive or anchored on a blockchain.
