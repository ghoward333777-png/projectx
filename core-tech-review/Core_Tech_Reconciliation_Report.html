2Claims kept, but corrected to what the specification supports

Your mid-review instruction was to correct errors without cutting content, so each of these claims stays in the draft, reworded to the defensible form the overview itself uses. "Cannot hallucinate" and "ungrounded assertions are structurally impossible" now read as the overview's structural-groundedness guarantee: an ungrounded assertion is structurally unavailable to the generation process, because the permissible output vocabulary is constrained to terms traceable to specific Fact Units — a stronger and more precise claim than an absolute that invites challenge. "Structurally incapable of harming humans" became "structurally constrained against causing harm," with all six supporting mechanisms retained and the measurable trial architecture added as evidence. "Does not rely on transformer embeddings" was qualified, because the overview specifies that each Fact Unit carries an embedding representation and that the ingestion domain is the sole location of any trained component; the corrected text says no answer rests on statistical generation, which is the claim that is actually true. "Becomes more stable as it grows because atomic universes naturally stabilize with size" conflicted with the specified capacity ceiling — a first-order phase boundary at which retrieval states disappear discontinuously — so stability at scale is now attributed to its engineered sources: energy-descent convergence, the conservative operating ceiling, and the elective unique-equilibrium regime. "Replaces GPU-heavy matrix computation… yielding cost savings" was restated as the architectural difference, with a sentence noting that specific compute-cost comparisons are deliberately not asserted, matching the overview's stated practice of citing only claims traceable to specified mechanisms. "Probable questions… saved and used forever" now carries the invalidation rule: a pre-computed answer is invalidated upon revision of any contributing record, so a served answer is never stale. "Self-evolving and capable of autonomous conceptual growth" was reframed as conceptual growth within the knowledge substrate under the non-bypassable human gate, since the overview makes every self-improvement finding a proposal requiring a named human's approval. And the broad brain analogies — edges as axons, exceptions as inhibitory microcircuits, "a functional analog of the brain," the hippocampus and prefrontal-cortex parallels — were kept as plain-language analogies but placed explicitly outside the five claimed correspondences, because the overview's Drafting Record deliberately declined to carry the brain-region mapping table and admits correspondences only under the Chapter 101 methodology.
