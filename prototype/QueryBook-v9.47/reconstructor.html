Scene Reconstructor — prose → a director-controlled clip

Paste a scene from a book, script, article, or synopsis. QueryBook extracts the scene (characters, setting, mood, beats), applies a style + cultural context, handles extra characters as silhouettes, and composes the exact Gemini prompts + a mood-matched music cue sheet. The clip is an interpretive visualization of the text, not an asserted fact; prompt building is deterministic.

🔑 Gemini / Veo video key

Paste your Google Gemini API key (with Veo access) to render real video. Stored only on this computer (local git-ignored file); never shown back or committed. Without it you get a dry-run prompt plan.
