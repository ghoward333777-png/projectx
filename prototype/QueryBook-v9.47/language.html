One click runs a Phase-1 agent on a bundled English corpus — it needs no internet and keeps working 24×7 until the gate opens. Add your own text/pages below to teach it faster.

Honest scope. Phase 1 (structural learning) and Phase 2 (semantic grounding) are built and run here. Phase 2 is fully deterministic — it grounds words from a bundled public-domain dictionary (Webster's 1913) and by self-grounding against your verified store, with no LLM and no internet; every meaning carries an auditable source. (LLMs are locked out of Phase 2 — a live API check can prove a connection works but never that a meaning is true. A future "suggestor" mode may propose meanings accepted only when they match the dictionary.) All four phases now run deterministically: Phase 3 learns the multilingual delta from a bundled bilingual dictionary, and Phase 4 analyzes pronunciation (G2P phonemes, syllables, stress) and can speak through your computer's built-in voice — no LLM anywhere. The full neural language and neural vocoder engines remain the roadmap embodiment. The grapheme→phoneme map is rule-seeded; gate thresholds are the prototype calibration.

Analyze & Speak Pronunciation: deterministic

Type a word in the selected language to see its pronunciation (IPA, syllables, stress) and hear it. This pronounces text — it does not translate it.

🎚️ Voice & style — pick a voice and steer tone, emotion, accent, pace

Google (Gemini): choose a voice above, then steer delivery. These become a natural-language instruction Gemini follows while speaking.

ElevenLabs: model + voice settings.

Build a custom Google voice from a sample. Record or upload a short, clean sample of the speaker's own voice; QueryBook builds a cloned voice you can then pick above. Requires the speaker's explicit consent — this is a biometric voice feature.

🗣️ Dialects — features & detection deterministic

Dialects are first-class linguistic systems here (Dialect Parameter Clusters). Choosing one above overlays its phonology on the pronunciation (deterministic, approximate) and steers the voice's accent. Rules below are rule-based and reproducible — no model asserts anything.

🔊 Realistic neural voices (optional) — connect Google or ElevenLabs

Add your own API key to use a realistic AI voice. Your key is stored only on this computer (a local, git-ignored file) and is never shown back or sent anywhere but the provider. The open-source engine stays the default and the offline fallback.

Pronunciation (IPA) works for every language with no install. To hear non-English languages, double-click GET-VOICES-WINDOWS.bat once — it installs the free espeak-ng voice engine so every language speaks with a real voice, offline. (English speaks already.)
