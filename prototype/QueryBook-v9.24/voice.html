Voice-first & accessible. Speak to QueryBook and it speaks back, in any supported language. Answers are announced for screen readers, controls are keyboard-operable (press Space to start/stop listening), and everything works hands-free in Converse mode. Your microphone and camera are used only in your browser — no audio or video is uploaded, and voice identity stays on this computer.

Two-way voice

Speak in your language; QueryBook understands it, answers from verified facts (or says it doesn't know), and speaks the answer back. Understanding uses your browser's speech recognition (Chrome or Edge); speaking uses the built-in / espeak-ng voices.

    Roadmap — audio-video “who is who”. Recognizing people by face and synchronizing it with the voice (audio-visual speaker identification) needs trained vision models and careful consent and privacy handling, so it is not included here. Today, “who is who” is done by voice above. The camera preview and sensor readouts are live; scene/face understanding is the roadmap embodiment.
    Covenant & privacy. Speech recognition, tone, and speaker identity are estimates, so they are never stored as verified facts — a spoken question runs through the grounded engine, which cites a source or honestly says it doesn’t know. Audio and video are processed only in your browser and are not uploaded; voiceprints live only on this computer and can be cleared at any time.
