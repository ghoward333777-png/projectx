Women-first safety, built into the product

  • Contact data filtering — phones, emails, links, and handles are erased from messages until a chat unlocks (30 days + 10 real conversations).
  • Slow-chat pacing — limited message size and frequency early on defeats the rapid-pressure playbook scammers rely on.
  • Red-flag detection — money requests, urgency pressure, and off-platform pushes raise a safety alert automatically (try typing "wire me money" in the chat demo).
  • Vetted venues only — partner venues are tagged for quiet, adult, date-compatible atmospheres.
  • VIP chaperones — professional bodyguard partners offer discreet escorts, safe-arrival verification, and emergency response.
  • Privacy-first popularity — nobody ever sees raw counts of who messaged or viewed you.

Red flags to watch for

  • Pushes to move the conversation off the platform right away.
  • Asks for money, gift cards, or financial details — in any form, ever.
  • Creates urgency: "decide now", "last chance", "don't tell anyone".
  • Refuses video verification or gives inconsistent personal details.
  • Excessive flattery and instant intensity before you have met.

First-date checklist

  • Meet at a vetted public venue from the app — never a private address.
  • Tell a friend where you are going and share your check-in plan.
  • Arrange your own transport there and back.
  • Keep your drink in sight at all times.
  • Book a VIP chaperone through the app if you want a professional nearby.
